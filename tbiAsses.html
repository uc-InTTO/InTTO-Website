What is TBI Assessment?

The TBI Assessment is a comprehensive evaluation program developed by DOST-PCIEERD to measure the performance and effectiveness of Technology Business Incubators in the Philippines.

This assessment helps TBIs identify strengths, address gaps, and continuously improve their services to support startup success and innovation ecosystem development.

DOST Certified
Industry Standard

Six key areas evaluated in the TBI Assessment

🎯

Strategic Management

Vision, mission, and strategic planning capabilities

  • Leadership structure
  • Goal alignment
  • Resource allocation
  • Risk management
🧑‍💼

Organizational Capacity

Team expertise, governance, and operational efficiency

  • Staff competencies
  • Governance framework
  • Standard operating procedures
  • Quality systems
💵

Financial Sustainability

Revenue streams, financial management, and sustainability

  • Revenue diversification
  • Financial planning
  • Cost management
  • Investment capacity
🧑‍💻

Service Delivery

Quality and range of services provided to incubatees

  • Service portfolio
  • Mentorship quality
  • Infrastructure access
  • Network connections

  • Startup success rate
  • Job creation
  • Economic impact
  • Innovation outputs

Monitoring & Evaluation

Data collection, analysis, and continuous improvement

  • KPI tracking
  • Data systems
  • Reporting mechanisms
  • Feedback integration

Assessment Process

Structured 6-step process for comprehensive evaluation

1

Self-Assessment

Complete comprehensive questionnaire about your TBI operations

2

Documentation Review

Submit relevant documents and operational data

3

Site Visit

On-site evaluation by DOST-certified assessors

4

Stakeholder Interviews

Interviews with staff, incubatees, and partners

5

Assessment Report

Detailed report with scores and recommendations

6

Improvement Plan

Customized roadmap for enhancing TBI performance
