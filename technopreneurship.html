Technopreneurship Training

Equipping educators and innovators with the skills to teach and implement technology-based entrepreneurship.

Overview

Our Technopreneurship Training program equips faculty members, students, and aspiring entrepreneurs with the knowledge and skills needed to teach and implement technology-based entrepreneurship. This comprehensive program combines theoretical frameworks with practical application to foster innovation and entrepreneurial thinking.

Who Should Attend

  • Faculty members developing courses
  • Educators integrating entrepreneurship
  • Innovation center coordinators
  • Professionals transitioning to mentoring

Training Modules

Foundations

Understanding the intersection of technology, innovation, and entrepreneurship.

Curriculum Design

Learn to design effective technopreneurship courses that inspire students.

Methodologies

Explore case studies, project-based learning, and experiential activities.

Best Practices

Stay current with the latest trends and strategies in tech entrepreneurship.
