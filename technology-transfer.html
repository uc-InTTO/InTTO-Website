Technology Transfer

Overview

Our Technology Transfer services facilitate the seamless transfer of technology and knowledge from the university to industry, enabling commercialization and wider application of research outputs. We bridge the gap between academic innovation and market implementation, creating value for researchers, institutions, and society.

Transfer Services

Technology Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation of research outputs to determine commercial viability and market potential.

Licensing Agreements

Negotiation and management of licensing deals that benefit both researchers and industry partners.

Industry Partnerships

Facilitation of collaborative research and development partnerships between academia and industry.

Spin-Off Support

Assistance in establishing spin-off companies to commercialize university-developed technologies.

The Transfer Process

  • Discovery & Disclosure: Identify and document innovative research outcomes with commercial potential
  • Protection: Secure appropriate intellectual property rights through patents, copyrights, or other mechanisms
  • Marketing: Promote technologies to potential licensees and industry partners
  • Licensing: Negotiate terms and execute agreements that benefit all stakeholders
  • Commercialization: Support the successful implementation and market entry of the technology
