IP Education

Comprehensive education on IP rights, protection strategies, and commercialization opportunities.

Overview

Our Intellectual Property Education program provides comprehensive education on IP rights, protection strategies, and commercialization opportunities. We help researchers, innovators, and entrepreneurs understand how to identify, protect, and leverage their intellectual property assets effectively in a competitive market.

Learning Outcomes

  • Understand IP Rights

    Learn different types of IP and protection mechanisms.

  • Commercial Potential

    Identify and evaluate the market value of innovations.

  • Application Process

    Navigate the IP filing and application process confidently.

  • Monetization Strategy

    Develop strategies for licensing and asset monetization.
