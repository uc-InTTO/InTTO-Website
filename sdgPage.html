About Our SDG Commitment

Transforming Innovation into Sustainable Impact

The University of the Cordilleras Innovation and Technology Transfer Office (UC InTTO) is committed to advancing the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals through evidence-based innovation, strategic partnerships, and community-centered entrepreneurship. Our startups, intellectual property, and programs are intentionally designed to address pressing local and global challenges while creating economic opportunities in the Cordilleras region and beyond.
