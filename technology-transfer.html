Technology Transfer

Bridging the gap between academic innovation and market implementation to create societal value.

Overview

Our Technology Transfer services facilitate the seamless transfer of technology and knowledge from the university to industry, enabling commercialization and wider application of research outputs. We bridge the gap between academic innovation and market implementation, creating value for researchers, institutions, and society.

The Transfer Process

1
Discovery & Disclosure

Identify and document innovative research outcomes with commercial potential.

2
Protection

Secure appropriate intellectual property rights through patents or copyrights.

3
Marketing

Promote technologies to potential licensees and industry partners.

4
Licensing

Negotiate terms and execute agreements that benefit all stakeholders.

5
Commercialization

Support the successful implementation and market entry of the technology.
