Train the Trainers Program

Comprehensive training program to develop world-class technopreneurship educators and mentors

Program Overview

The Train the Trainers (T3) Program is designed to equip faculty members, industry practitioners, and ecosystem builders with the knowledge, skills, and tools to effectively teach technopreneurship and mentor aspiring entrepreneurs.

This 10-day intensive program combines theoretical foundations with practical application, ensuring participants can implement learnings in their respective institutions.

📅

Duration

10 days

Who Should Join?

  • TBI staff and managers
  • Industry mentors and advisors
  • Government extension workers
  • NGO program implementers
  • Corporate trainers
