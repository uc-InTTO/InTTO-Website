Technopreneurship Training

Overview

Our Technopreneurship Training program equips faculty members, students, and aspiring entrepreneurs with the knowledge and skills needed to teach and implement technology-based entrepreneurship. This comprehensive program combines theoretical frameworks with practical application to foster innovation and entrepreneurial thinking.

Training Modules

Foundations of Technopreneurship

Understanding the intersection of technology, innovation, and entrepreneurship in today's digital economy.

Curriculum Development

Learn to design and implement effective technopreneurship courses that engage and inspire students.

Teaching Methodologies

Explore innovative teaching methods including case studies, project-based learning, and experiential activities.

Industry Best Practices

Stay current with the latest trends, tools, and strategies in technology entrepreneurship.

Who Should Attend

  • Faculty members looking to develop or enhance technopreneurship courses
  • Educators interested in integrating entrepreneurship into their teaching
  • Innovation and entrepreneurship center coordinators
  • Professionals transitioning to teaching or mentoring roles
