## Introduction

### What is the InTTO Website?

The **InTTO Website** is the official platform of the University of the Cordilleras Innovation & Technology Transfer Office. It serves as your gateway to discover innovative startups, stay updated on events and programs, and explore opportunities for collaboration and support in the innovation ecosystem.

### Who Is This Manual For?

This manual is designed for:
- **Students** exploring innovation opportunities
- **Entrepreneurs** seeking incubation support
- **Researchers** looking for collaboration
- **Industry Partners** interested in technology transfer
- **General Public** wanting to learn about UC's innovation ecosystem

### What Can You Do on the Website?

✅ **Discover Startups** - Browse innovative startups incubated at UC  
✅ **Stay Informed** - Read latest news and upcoming events  
✅ **Explore Programs** - Learn about incubation and support services  
✅ **Apply for Support** - Submit applications for various programs  
✅ **Connect** - Get in touch with the InTTO team  

### System Requirements

- **Browser:** Any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- **Internet Connection:** Active internet connection
- **Device:** Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **JavaScript:** Must be enabled in your browser

## Browsing Startups

### How to View All Startups

1. **Click "Startups"** in the main navigation menu
2. You'll see a grid of startup cards, each showing:
   - **Logo** - Visual identity of the startup
   - **Name** - Startup name and tagline
   - **Industry** - Technology sector or category
   - **Brief Description** - What the startup does
   - **SDG Icons** - Sustainable Development Goals they address

### Using Filters to Find Specific Startups

Use the filter options at the top of the startups page:

- **SDG Filter** - Find startups working on specific UN Sustainable Development Goals (1-17)
- **Industry Filter** - Search by sector (e.g., HealthTech, AgriTech, EdTech, etc.)
- **Status Filter** - View active startups, graduated companies, or those in pre-incubation
- **Search Bar** - Type keywords to find specific startups

### Viewing Detailed Startup Information

1. **Click on any startup card** to open its full profile
2. Explore the detailed sections:

   **Overview Tab**
   - Mission statement and vision
   - Detailed description of the innovation
   - Problem being solved
   - Unique value proposition

   **Team Tab**
   - Founders and their backgrounds
   - Team size and composition
   - Key personnel profiles

   **Technology Tab**
   - Technology Readiness Level (TRL)
   - Technical specifications
   - Innovation highlights
   - Development stage

   **Impact Tab**
   - Related Sustainable Development Goals
   - Social impact metrics
   - Environmental benefits
   - Community reach

   **Achievements Tab**
   - Awards and recognitions
   - Milestones reached
   - Success stories
   - Media coverage

   **Contact Tab**
   - Official website link
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - Social media profiles

3. **View the image gallery** - Browse photos of the product, team, or facilities
4. **Read related startups** - Discover similar innovations at the bottom of the page
5. **Share on social media** - Use share buttons to spread the word

### Tips for Exploring Startups

💡 **Tip 1:** Use the SDG filter to find startups aligned with causes you care about  
💡 **Tip 2:** Click on founder names to potentially connect via provided contact info  
💡 **Tip 3:** Bookmark favorite startups using your browser's bookmark feature

## Reading News & Events

### Browsing News Articles

1. **Navigate to "Events"** in the main menu or scroll to the news section on the homepage
2. You'll see news cards with:
   - Featured image
   - Headline
   - Publication date

## Applying for Programs

### Incubation Program Application

Ready to apply for incubation? Follow these steps:

#### Step 1: Check Eligibility

Before applying, ensure you meet the requirements:
- Have a viable business idea or early-stage startup
- Be affiliated with UC or have a UC partner
- Demonstrate innovation and scalability potential
- Be committed to the incubation program duration

#### Step 2: Access the Application Form

1. Go to the **"Incubation Program"** page
2. Read through the program details and benefits
3. Click the **"Apply Now"** button
4. You'll be directed to the online application form

#### Step 3: Complete the Application

The application is divided into four sections:

**Section 1: Personal Information**
- Your full name
- Email address (will be used for all communications)
- Phone number
- Affiliation (UC student/faculty/alumni/external)
- Department or organization

**Section 2: Startup/Innovation Details**
- Proposed startup name
- Industry or sector
- Development stage (idea, prototype, testing, early sales)
- Detailed description of your innovation
- Problem you're solving
- Your proposed solution
- Target market and customers
- Competitive advantage

**Section 3: Team Information**
- Team members (names and roles)
- Team size
- Relevant skills and experience
- Previous startup experience (if any)
- Educational background

**Section 4: Support Needs**
- Type of support you need (workspace, mentorship, funding, etc.)
- Timeline for your project
- Funding requirements (if any)
- Specific resources needed
- Expected outcomes

#### Step 4: Review and Submit

1. **Review all information** carefully in the summary section
2. **Make corrections** if needed by going back to specific sections
3. **Accept terms and conditions**
4. **Click "Submit Application"**
5. **Save your confirmation number** for reference

#### Step 5: After Submission

✅ You'll receive an automatic confirmation email  
✅ Application will be reviewed within 7-14 business days  
✅ InTTO may contact you for additional information  
✅ You'll be notified of the decision via email  
✅ If accepted, you'll receive onboarding instructions  

### Other Program Applications

For other programs (workshops, training, mentorship), the application process is similar:

1. **Find the program** in the Events or Services section
2. **Click "Register" or "Apply"**
3. **Fill out the form** with required information
4. **Submit and await confirmation**

### Application Tips

📝 **Be Clear and Concise** - Explain your idea in simple terms  
📝 **Show Passion** - Demonstrate your commitment and enthusiasm  
📝 **Be Realistic** - Provide honest assessments of your stage and needs  
📝 **Proofread** - Check for errors before submitting  
📝 **Provide Evidence** - Include links, prototypes, or data if available

### A. Understanding SDGs

**What are Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)?**

The UN Sustainable Development Goals are 17 global goals designed to achieve a better and more sustainable future for all. InTTO tracks how startups and innovations contribute to these goals.

**The 17 SDGs:**

1. **No Poverty** - End poverty in all forms
2. **Zero Hunger** - End hunger and achieve food security
3. **Good Health and Well-being** - Ensure healthy lives
4. **Quality Education** - Ensure inclusive and equitable education
5. **Gender Equality** - Achieve gender equality
6. **Clean Water and Sanitation** - Ensure availability of water
7. **Affordable and Clean Energy** - Ensure access to sustainable energy
8. **Decent Work and Economic Growth** - Promote sustained economic growth
9. **Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure** - Build resilient infrastructure
10. **Reduced Inequalities** - Reduce inequality within and among countries
11. **Sustainable Cities and Communities** - Make cities inclusive and sustainable
12. **Responsible Consumption and Production** - Ensure sustainable consumption
13. **Climate Action** - Take urgent action on climate change
14. **Life Below Water** - Conserve oceans and marine resources
15. **Life on Land** - Protect terrestrial ecosystems
16. **Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions** - Promote peaceful societies
17. **Partnerships for the Goals** - Strengthen global partnerships

### B. Technology Readiness Levels (TRL)

**What is TRL?**

Technology Readiness Level is a measurement system used to assess the maturity of evolving technologies.

**TRL Scale:**

- **TRL 1:** Basic principles observed and reported
- **TRL 2:** Technology concept and application formulated
- **TRL 3:** Experimental proof of concept
- **TRL 4:** Technology validated in laboratory
- **TRL 5:** Technology validated in relevant environment
- **TRL 6:** Technology demonstrated in relevant environment
- **TRL 7:** System prototype demonstration in operational environment
- **TRL 8:** System complete and qualified
- **TRL 9:** Actual system proven in operational environment

Higher TRL means the technology is closer to market readiness.

### D. Glossary of Terms

**Incubation** - A program that provides support services to startups including workspace, mentorship, and resources.

**Startup** - A newly established business, typically focused on innovation and rapid growth.

**IP (Intellectual Property)** - Creations of the mind, such as inventions, designs, and artistic works, that can be legally protected.

**Patent** - Legal protection for an invention, giving exclusive rights to make, use, or sell the invention.

**TRL** - Technology Readiness Level; a measure of technology maturity.

**SDG** - Sustainable Development Goal; UN goals for global development.

**Technology Transfer** - The process of transferring scientific findings from research to commercial application.

**Pitch** - A brief presentation of a business idea or innovation to potential investors or partners.

**Prototype** - An early sample or model of a product built to test a concept.

**Scalability** - The capability of a business to grow without being hampered by its structure or available resources.
