IP Education

Overview

Our Intellectual Property Education program provides comprehensive education on IP rights, protection strategies, and commercialization opportunities. We help researchers, innovators, and entrepreneurs understand how to identify, protect, and leverage their intellectual property assets effectively.

Learning Outcomes

  • Understand the different types of IP rights and when to use each protection mechanism
  • Identify and evaluate the commercial potential of your innovations
  • Navigate the IP application process with confidence
  • Develop strategies for licensing and monetizing your IP assets
